A note from the founder

From your phone into the plan.

Our phones already know where we are, when we’re free and what we love. Yet making plans with friends still means a group chat that goes nowhere, calendars nobody checks, and a “we should hang out soon” that never happens. Busy lives get in the way of the moments that matter most.

Loop is built to change that. It learns the people and places you love, notices when everyone is free and suggests a plan that works for all of you. Then it keeps looking out for the next thing worth your time, so you can actually be there for it.

Social networks were supposed to bring us closer, but most of them keep us on our phones. Loop does the opposite. It brings you from your phone into the plan, the way social networks should.

We’re starting in Dallas, doing one thing really well. As Loop grows, it will take on more of the planning and remembering that busy lives crowd out, until it becomes the smart assistant that keeps you in the loop with everything you care about.

I’d love for you to grow with it.

NickFounder, Loop
