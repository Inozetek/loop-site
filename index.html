What Loop does

Less planning. More showing up.

Three things, done well, in one app.

01 · Find

What’s worth doing, right now.

Real places and events near you, picked for your taste, with today’s hours and a time that fits your day.

02 · Plan

Fair to everyone.

Loop sees who’s free and suggests a spot that works for the whole group. One invite, one plan.

03 · Always on

Always looking out for you.

New shows, openings and spots you’d hate to miss, caught the moment they fit your free time. Your best hours go to the things that make life better, with a reminder and the route when it’s time to go.

04 · Made for the whole group
The next plan that makes sense for everyone.

Loop learns each person’s taste, budget and favorite spots, then suggests the event the whole group will actually want. Never farther or pricier than it needs to be, and no back-and-forth to get there.

05 · It learns you

The longer you use it, the more it gets you.

Loop learns from what you actually do, not what you tell it. Every place you visit, every event you save, every plan you make sharpens its sense of your taste. Over time it stops being an app you search and becomes the guide you trust. Until one morning you wake up to a list of things you didn’t know you needed, on a day you couldn’t have planned better yourself.
